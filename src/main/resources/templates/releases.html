September 02, 2026

1.34.0

  • Validates certificate chains according to BSI TR-02103
  • Updates transformation of country codes
  • Adjusts PID in credential configuration id pid-mso-mdoc_3-beta
    • Removes place_of_birth.no_place_info and expiry_date
    • Adds raw_eid_birth_date with partially unknown birthdate
    • Updates birth_date to correspond to ISO 8601 (partially unknown birthdates get set to the last day of month / last month of year)
  • Adjusts PID in credential configuration id pid-sd-jwt_3-beta
    • Removes place_of_birth.no_place_info and date_of_expiry
    • Adds raw_eid_birth_date with partially unknown birthdate
    • Updates birthdate to correspond to ISO 8601 (partially unknown birthdates get set to the last day of month / last month of year)
  • Chain Revocation
    • Adds foundational support for revoking PMTs and PID Credentials upon Wallet Unit (Instance) revocation
    • Adds foundational support for synchronizing monitored Wallet Provider Status Lists
    • Verifies synchronized (fetched) Wallet Provider Status List Tokens
